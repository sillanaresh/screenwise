Privacy policy

Screenwise doesn't collect your data.

Last updated 19 November 2026.

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few things on your iPhone so it works the way you left it:

  • Theatres you save.
  • Movies and theatres you looked at recently.
  • The name, mobile number and email you choose to type in, if any. These are optional.
  • Small settings, such as tips you have already seen.

This stays on your phone. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app downloads

The app downloads the list of movies and the list of Hyderabad theatres from GitHub, and movie pictures from The Movie Database (TMDB). Like any website, these services see your device's internet address when the app downloads from them. They don't receive anything about you from Screenwise. Their own privacy policies apply: GitHub and TMDB.

Feedback and corrections

When you send feedback or a correction, the app opens your own email app with a message you can read and change before sending. If you send it, we receive what you wrote and your email address. We use it only to reply and to fix the theatre or movie details. We don't share it or add you to any list.

Booking tickets

Book tickets opens BookMyShow or District. Screenwise doesn't sell tickets and doesn't see what you do there. Their privacy policies apply.

Children

Screenwise is for a general audience. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
