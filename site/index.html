Screenwise

Pick a movie. We'll pick the theatre.

Screenwise ranks Hyderabad's top cinema screens for the movie you want to see, by picture, sound and screen size.

How it works

  • Every screen gets a score out of 100 for each movie. The same screen can rank differently for another movie, because movies are shot in different shapes and formats.
  • You can look around each hall in 3D and see how big the picture looks from your row.
  • Every spec shows where it came from. Anything we haven't confirmed is marked.
  • The movie list updates every day. Theatres are checked every week or two.
